# Goofy Ahh Maze
UAS (IF580-G) Computer Graphic & Animation

Goofy Ahh Maze is a heart-pounding survival horror game set in a series of randomized mazes where danger lurks in every shadow. With only a flashlight and a pistol loaded with a single bullet, you must navigate through three increasingly terrifying levels, each guarded by a different, relentless hunter.

Your mission is simple—find the key hidden somewhere in the maze and unlock the exit door at the far end of the map. But survival is anything but easy. The maze reshuffles every time you play, keeping you disoriented and on edge. Meanwhile, your flashlight’s battery drains over time, forcing you to search for scattered batteries to keep the darkness at bay.

Resource management is critical. Ammunition is scarce, and every shot counts. Confront the hunters head-on or evade them as they stalk you through the twisting corridors. With each level introducing a new, deadlier predator, the tension builds until the final escape—or your ultimate demise.

Key Features:
- Three Unique Levels: Face progressively challenging mazes and hunters, each with distinct behaviors.
- Randomized Maze Design: Every playthrough offers a different layout, ensuring replayability and constant suspense.
- Survival Resource Management: Keep your flashlight powered with scattered batteries and find ammo to stay alive.
- Limited Starting Resources: Begin with only a flashlight and 1 bullet, forcing you to scavenge carefully.
- Persistent Threats: Hunters respawn, ensuring no place is truly safe.

Can you survive all three levels and escape the maze, or will the hunters claim you as their prey? Find the key, face the fear, and fight for your life—before the darkness swallows you whole.
